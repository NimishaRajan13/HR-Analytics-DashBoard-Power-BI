
# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee demographics, workforce distribution, and employee attrition. The dashboard provides insights into HR metrics to help understand employee turnover patterns and support data-driven human resource decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total and active employee counts.
- Measure employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Understand attrition across departments and salary slabs.
- Analyze employee age groups and gender distribution.
- Explore job satisfaction across different job roles.
- Examine the relationship between employee experience and attrition.
- Compare employee distribution across departments.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard development and data visualization
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation, if used
- DAX – KPI calculations and measures, if used

📈 Dashboard Features

1. Employee Overview
Displays key HR metrics, including total employees, active employees, attrition count, attrition rate, average age, and average experience.

2. Attrition by Department
Visualizes employee attrition across departments to identify areas with higher employee turnover.

3. Attrition by Salary Slab
Compares employee attrition across salary ranges to explore possible patterns between salary levels and employee retention.

4. Attrition by Job Role and Job Satisfaction
Presents job satisfaction ratings across different job roles to help understand employee satisfaction patterns.

5. Age Group Distribution
Shows the distribution of employees across different age groups, including 18–25, 26–35, 36–45, 46–55, and 55+.

6. Attrition by Gender
Compares attrition counts across male and female employees.

7. Attrition Trend by Experience
Visualizes attrition across years of work experience to explore workforce retention patterns.

8. Department-Wise Employee Count
Compares employee headcount across departments to understand workforce distribution.
9. Interactive Department Filter
Allows users to filter dashboard visuals by department for a more focused analysis.

💡 Key Insights

- The dashboard reports an overall attrition rate of 16.3%.
- The 26–35 age group represents the largest employee age group, with 588 employees.
- The Operations department has the highest employee count in the department-wise chart, with 512 employees.
- The dashboard enables comparison of attrition by salary slab, department, gender, and job role.

*These observations are descriptive and do not establish the causes of employee attrition.*
